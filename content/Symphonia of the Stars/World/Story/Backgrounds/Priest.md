## Overview

**Priests** are the cornerstone of all religious civilization, the powers blessed upon them by the gods they serve are immense and powerful enough to be able to ward off evil spirits such as the undead or accursed Starbounds.

There are many type of **Priests** and depending on the god they serve, such as;

1. Priest of Storms
	The Priest of storms are responsible in keeping ships afloat, they serve the goddess [[Seralyne, Goddess of the Seas|Seralyne]]. They hail mostly from [[Kingdom of the Seas, Velis Mare|The Sea Kingdom, Velis Mare]]. Some of the blessings they have obtained are;
	1. Water Breathing ◎
	2. Divination ○
	3. Heal ◎
	4. Exorcism ○

2. Priest of War
	The Priest of war are responsible as mostly combat medics, they serve the god [[Kaelar, God of Progress|Kaelar]]. they come from the [[Garyuda Empire]] and mostly work under another name, Paladins. Their skills are;
	1. Divine Smite ○
	2. Divination ○
	3. Heal ◎
	4. Exorcism ◎

3. Priest of the Night
	The Priest of the Night are responsible mostly acting as a true priest, working under [[Nadira, Goddess of Death|Nadira]], though some tend to be weirder than the standard priest, such as the Idol group ONYX/R or ONYX/D, they work as both Idol and Priest, regularly exorcising Ghouls, Spirits, Demonic Influence, or Dark Cults with large sound based AOE attacks. Their skills are;
	1. Night Blind ○
	2. Divination ○
	3. Heal ○
	4. Exorcism ○
	5. Banshee Shout ○

4. Priest of the Sun
	The Priest of the Sun are like their counterpart, they function like true priests working under [[Solanir, God of the Sun|Solanir's Church]], they're mostly found in [[Principality of the Suneaters]], their holy city is akin to that of the Vatican, with the downside of them not being able to work in any other field. Their skills are;
	1. Flashbang ○
	2. Divination ◎
	3. Heal ◎
	4. Exorcism ○
	5. Divine Smite ○
