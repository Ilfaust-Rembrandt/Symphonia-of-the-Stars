## Overview

The **Sagittarius Pathway** is about hunting a certain target until it's end, whether it is a concept, living being, or even god himself. They specialize in tracking, invisibility, and archery. They are also proficient in cavalry combat and strategies.

At high Sequences, they can borrow from the powers of their sister constellations, such as [[Pathway of the Hunter, Orion Constellation|Orion]], [[Pathway of the Virgin, Virgo Constellation|Virgo]], and [[Pathway of the Maiden, Libra Constellation|Libra]], to enhance their hunts. They are also able to hunt stronger concepts such as time itself or kill ideas from the human mind.

The Starbounds of this pathway is able to branch their pathway starting at sequence 4.

The Above the sequence of the Sagittarius Pathway is <font color="#95b3d7">"The First Star"</font>.

### Sequence 9, Polaris

**NEW Abilities**

**Passive**:
- **Enhanced Eyesight**: At first, those who drink the potion for the Constellation Sagittarius will notice that their eyesight has become sharper, those that wore glasses would no longer need it. Their eyes would be able to zoom in and out naturally like lenses.
- **Hunter's Instinct**: Starbounds of this constellation would be able to sense danger up to a 50m radius, and are able to fire projectile based weaponries with great precision.
- **Undetected**: Starbounds of this constellation is able to walk silently and are virtually undetected, however a far cry from true invisibility.

**Star Chart**:
- **Starstruck arrows**: When wielding a bow, faint starlight will envelop your arrow, granting it the ability to ricochet off of objects or walls up to two bounces an arrow. (2 Uses per short rest)
- **The North Star**: Mark a target. Once a day you can use **The North Star** to track the target, even from impossibly far ranges.

### Sequence 8, Luminous Archer

**NEW Abilities**

**Passive**:
- **Radiant Vision**: Able to see under complete darkness as if under sunlight. Hidden targets would glimmer with faint starlight.
- **Arcana Draw**: When wielding a bow, your body adapts to the strength and weight, making you be able to wield them with ease as if light as a feather.
- **Stellar Alignment**: When focusing and drawing a bow, faint trajectorial lines of starlight would appear visible only to Sagittarius Starbounds.

**Star Chart**:
- **Meteor Storm**: Imbue any type of projectile with the same starlight as **Starstruck arrows**. Increase the bounce to 6 bounces.
- **Sunlight Radiance**: Once a day, you can fire multiple projectiles, they will glow faintly and home towards the target, granted the target was marked before hand.
- **Mark of the Hunted**: Mark an enemy, any projectile you or an ally fire towards that enemy will have increased damage, apply **Mark of the Hunted** to the enemy.
