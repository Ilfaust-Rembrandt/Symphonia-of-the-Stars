## Overview

**Sagittarius** Pathway is the pathway of many teachers, police, and even those at the royal guards, the main focus of this pathway is the teachings of healing techniques, hunting, and horse-riding.

They are teachers and mentors to most other Pathways, and thus most of the Starbounds of the Sagittarius Constellation are those with positions to teach.
