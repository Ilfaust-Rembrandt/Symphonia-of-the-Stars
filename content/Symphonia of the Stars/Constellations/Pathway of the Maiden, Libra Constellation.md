## Overview

**Libra, Pathway of the Maiden** is the Constellation pathway of many of those who pursued the route of justice, such as lawyers, prosecutors, kings, queens, and many more.

The **Libra** Pathway is about delivering justice, whether true means such as a fair trial, or through your own hands, such as vigilantism.

Those of the Libra Pathway usually become judges of their own fate, actors of vengeance, and are incredibly fair.
